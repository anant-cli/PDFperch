How to Add a Watermark to a PDF Without Adobe — Free

By Anant · May 2026

Published May 2026 | 📌 PDF, Guide

Watermarks are used to mark drafts, indicate confidentiality, assert ownership, or deter unauthorised distribution. Adobe Acrobat charges a monthly fee for this feature. ConvertPDF does it for free, in your browser, without uploading your file anywhere.

Step-by-Step: Watermark a PDF

  1. Open the Watermark PDF tool.
  2. Upload your PDF by dragging it onto the drop zone or clicking to browse.
  3. Type your watermark text — "CONFIDENTIAL", "DRAFT", your name, or anything else.
  4. Customise: choose font size, opacity (how transparent the watermark appears), rotation angle, and colour.
  5. Click Add Watermark and download the result.

Common Watermark Use Cases

  • DRAFT — mark in-progress documents shared for review so recipients don't treat them as final.
  • CONFIDENTIAL — remind recipients of the document's sensitivity and discourage casual sharing.
  • SAMPLE — share previews of work (reports, templates, designs) without giving away the finished product.
  • Your name or website URL — mark PDFs you publish so readers know their source.
  • DO NOT COPY — deter reproduction of licensed documents.

Tips for Effective Watermarks

  • Opacity 20–35% is usually ideal — visible enough to notice but not so heavy it obscures the content.
  • 45° rotation is the classic diagonal watermark. 0° gives a horizontal stamp look.
  • Large font size (80–120pt) ensures the watermark spans the whole page on A4/Letter.
  • Use a light grey or pale colour for the subtlest effect; bright red for maximum visibility.

Is a Watermark Permanent?

The text watermark is embedded into the PDF page content — it cannot be removed without specialist software. However, for truly sensitive documents, consider also password protecting the PDF to prevent editing entirely.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The watermark is applied using pdf-lib entirely within your browser. Your file never leaves your device.

Can I watermark all pages at once?

Yes — the watermark is applied to every page of the document automatically.

Can I add an image watermark (logo)?

The current tool supports text watermarks only. Image watermark support is planned for a future update.

Will the watermark work on password-protected PDFs?

No — you'll need to remove the password first using the PDF Unlock tool, watermark it, then re-protect if needed.
