Anant

Developer · Privacy Advocate · Creator of PDFperch

As the founder and lead developer of PDFperch, I build browser-based document tools that prioritise privacy and transparency. PDFperch is a client-side suite of utilities for converting, merging, encrypting, and exporting documents without uploading file content to external servers.

Professional Focus

My work is guided by a commitment to data minimisation and user control. PDFperch is built on standard open web technologies and trusted open-source libraries. Because all file processing occurs within the browser, your documents remain on your device and are not shared with third parties.

Technical Approach

The service uses browser-native APIs and libraries such as pdf-lib, Mammoth.js, and KaTeX to deliver practical document functionality. This architecture allows us to provide powerful tools while maintaining a strict no-upload, no-tracking policy.

Our Mission

PDFperch was created to offer an alternative to online document converters that require accounts, file uploads, or data collection. The goal is to ensure that users can complete everyday document tasks quickly, securely, and privately.

Contact and Transparency

If you have questions, feedback, or wish to report a security issue, please contact us at pdfperch@gmail.com. We do not use analytics or tracking, and we do not collect personal browsing behaviour through PDFperch.
