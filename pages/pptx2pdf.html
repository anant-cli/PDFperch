PPTX to PDF converter

Turn a PowerPoint PPTX deck into a PDF without uploading it. The tool renders each slide as a static image and lays those images onto A4 or Letter PDF pages.

Best for: slide handouts, sharing decks, and printable notes.
Input: PPTX presentation files.
Output: PDF file.

How it works

The converter reads the PPTX file in your browser, renders the visible slides, captures each slide as an image, and places those images into a PDF. You can choose one slide per page or compact handout layouts with two, four, or six slides per page.

Privacy

Your presentation is processed locally. It is not uploaded to ConvertPDF servers, and the generated PDF is created on your device.

Limitations

Browser-based PowerPoint rendering is useful but not identical to Microsoft PowerPoint export. Animations, videos, audio, embedded macros, protected decks, unusual fonts, and complex effects may not appear exactly as they do in PowerPoint. Always review the PDF before sending official material.
