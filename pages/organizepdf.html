Organize PDF Pages

Reorder, rotate, and delete individual pages in any PDF using a drag-and-drop visual interface — completely free and 100% private. Your file never leaves your device.

Best for: Rearranging scanned documents, deleting blank or unwanted pages, and fixing upside-down pages.
Input: Any PDF document.
Output: A new reorganized PDF, downloaded instantly.
Limit: Works entirely in-browser; very large PDFs (>200 pages) may be slower.

How to Organize Your PDF

1

Upload PDF

Drag and drop your PDF into the tool above, or click to browse and select it from your device.

2

Rearrange Pages

Drag page thumbnails to reorder them. Rotate individual pages, or click the delete button to remove any page.

3

Save Organized PDF

Click “Save Organized PDF” to download the updated document with your changes applied.

Features

  • Drag-and-drop reorder: Intuitively drag page thumbnails into any order you need.
  • Rotate pages: Rotate any page 90° clockwise with one click. Useful for fixing scanned pages that came out sideways.
  • Delete pages: Remove any unwanted page from the final output.
  • Visual thumbnails: See a preview of each page before saving so there are no surprises.
  • No upload needed: Everything runs locally in your browser using PDF-Lib and PDF.js.

Privacy & Security

All PDF processing in this tool is handled by JavaScript libraries running inside your browser tab. PDFperch servers never receive your file, its name, or any of its contents. You can verify this by checking the Network tab in your browser's developer tools — you will see no upload traffic while using the tool.
