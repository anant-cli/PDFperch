PDF to Word

Convert selectable PDF text into an editable DOCX file. The conversion runs in your browser, so your file is not uploaded to ConvertPDF.

Best for: text PDFs, reports, notes, and forms with selectable text.
Input: PDF documents.
Output: Word DOCX documents.

Privacy
